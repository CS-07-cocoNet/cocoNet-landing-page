How cocoNet Works

From a Leaf Photo to a Nationwide Disease Map

Upload a coconut leaf photo and CocoGuard detects WCWLD and CCI, then shares every result in one central database that farmers and authorities can both see.

01
Any Phone

Snap or Upload a Leaf

Farmers photograph a coconut leaflet, upper surface for wilt and lower surface for caterpillars, or upload one from the gallery.

Leaf Photo JPEG • Camera or Gallery
02
Deep Learning

AI Detects WCWLD & CCI

Transfer-learning CNNs classify wilt and grade its severity, while Mask R-CNN and YOLO measure caterpillar damage and count larvae.
