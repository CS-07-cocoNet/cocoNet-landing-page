FARMER STORIES

Insights from the farming community

Summarise real research or interview findings and identify the source where appropriate.

01 / 06

  • Rhinoceros beetle Demo
    ★★★★★
    “By the time I spotted the V-shaped cuts in the crowns, the beetle had already damaged six young fronds on my best palms. I still copy whatever the neighbours use, because nobody has shown me what actually works.”

    How cocoNet helps: photo-based pest identification flags crown damage early and returns verified management steps the same day.

    Sunil Jayasuriya Smallholder farmer · Kuliyapitiya, Kurunegala
  • Grey leaf blight Demo
    ★★★★★
    “Grey leaf blight spreads fast during the inter-monsoon, and from the ground it looks almost like drought stress. Last year I sprayed copper across the whole block without knowing if it was right.”

    How cocoNet helps: percentage-scored predictions show what the model sees and how confident it is, with targeted guidance instead of blanket spraying.

    Nuwan Bandara Commercial farmer · Matale
  • Black-headed caterpillar Demo
    ★★★★★
    “For almost two seasons the lower leaflets dried out and I thought it was just the dry weather. When the officer finally identified the black-headed caterpillar, the best time to treat it had already passed.”

    How cocoNet helps: leaf-level identification with confidence scores, plus a picture-based disease library that can be read in Sinhala or Tamil.

    Kamala Wijekoon Smallholder farmer · Puttalam
  • Bud rot & wilt Demo
    ★★★★★
    “We lost more than forty palms to rot after the rains — the spindle collapses and by then there is nothing left to save. Reporting took weeks, so the neighbouring plots went down one after another.”

    How cocoNet helps: early detection with automatic observation recording speeds up reporting, and the district dashboard shows outbreaks forming sooner.

    Priyantha de Silva Estate owner · Matara
  • Regional reporting Demo
    ★★★★★
    “Most disease reports still reach me by a phone call, sometimes two weeks after the first symptoms appear. I only see a pattern once three or four farmers call about the same thing.”

    How cocoNet helps: the web dashboard plots farmer-submitted observations by district, so officers can monitor disease patterns and plan inspections on evidence.

    Anoma Rajapaksha Agricultural Officer · Puttalam district
  • Research data Demo
    ★★★★★
    “Every study begins with hunting through scattered photos, notes and harvest books that were never recorded in a usable form. Without geotagged observations we cannot connect disease pressure to location or season.”

    How cocoNet helps: verified observations build a geotagged, filterable disease database that researchers can query and export for every future study.

    Dr. Chandana Perera Agricultural researcher · Coconut Research Institute

The stories above are illustrative demo content created for layout review, written against the interview guide in the cocoNet project proposal (farmer, agricultural officer and researcher personas).
